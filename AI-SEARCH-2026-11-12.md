## Content and implementation

`content/search-guides.json` contains all 21 localized articles and their publication/modification dates. `scripts/build-landing.py` generates their visible answers, instructions, examples, Article/Breadcrumb metadata, canonical URLs, reciprocal language alternates, related links and official store badge pairs. Homepage questions in `content/seo.json` now include recurring bills and pending tips. Both homepage badge placements, approved hero copy, existing attribution campaigns and localized media remain intact. The sitemap grows from 54 to 68 actual pages. `llms.txt` adds the guide links and concise product facts; robots.txt continues to allow crawling and was not changed.

Product facts were checked against the released 1.2-series help, ADR-0018 and the iOS/Android amount-confirmation logic:

- A Loop saves a schedule, payer and split. Due periods require review and recording; they are not automatic payments. A free Book allows 3 active Loops.
- Pro supports estimated expenses. Saved and synced estimates already affect balances. Confirming the final total updates the original expense, rather than adding another expense. Equal, percentage and shares splits are supported; exact-amount estimates are not. Members can confirm an existing estimate without their own Pro subscription.
- Pending is a user-marked amount, not a detected card/bank status. LoopSplit does not detect tip posting or calculate tips automatically.
- Receipt recognition suggests amount, merchant and date when available. Pro scans automatically; free users request a scan and have 3 scans total per account across devices. Free scanning needs a connection for quota confirmation. Photo attachments are free; saved shared photos upload to the Book, while OCR runs on-device. Scanning does not allocate receipt line items or save an expense automatically.

Examples are illustrative USD amounts, not user records: rent plus internet `(1,800 + 60) / 2 = 930`; an estimated meal `120 / 3 = 40` confirmed as `132 / 3 = 44`; a scanned receipt `90 / 3 = 30`. All assume no other entries or repayments when stating money owed.
