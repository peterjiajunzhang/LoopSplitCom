Review the specific excluded URLs before changing canonicals or deleting pages. Build useful worked examples around observed roommate/utility queries, rather than producing near-duplicate keyword pages.

Keep the existing Apple and Play campaigns. Review actual store campaign acquisition results separately from Google clicks. No website analytics or Install Referrer SDK was added, so a website-to-install user-level funnel has not been established. No advertising spend or store listing changes were made. Consider a small advertising test only after the relevant store attribution reporting is available and a budget is explicitly approved.

Titles and snippets help explain relevance and clicks; Google chooses the final presentation. No fabricated ratings, keyword stuffing or FAQ rich-result promise is used.
