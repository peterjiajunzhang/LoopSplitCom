# iOS 1.0.0 help center — October 22, 2026

Scope: public user instructions for iPhone/iPad 1.0.0, preserving /support/ and the existing contact email.

Seven guides cover setup/invites, expenses/splits/income/corrections, Loops and due periods, balances/partial repayments, receipts/scanning/estimates, free/Pro/subscriptions, and account/offline/notifications/deletion. The help hub has progressive-enhancement topic filtering, quick answers and a contact section. Articles have stable topic anchors, steps and expected outcomes, with arithmetic examples for shares and repayments.

Content was checked against the public 1.0.0 App Store listing and current iOS source on origin/main, rather than the older feature/web-mcp working checkout. In particular: due Loops are recorded in To Do; estimates already affect balances; scanning only prefills the form; free scans are three total per account across devices; repayments never move actual money. No Android operating instructions or unreleased web features are included.

Validation: all local HTML paths and fragments resolve, image alt attributes and unique IDs checked, all support pages have one h1, sitemap parses, JavaScript syntax and whitespace checks pass. Browser exercised receipt-topic filtering, zero-results feedback, clearing search, article navigation and a repayment section anchor. Narrow-screen system-dark screenshots inspected. Light mode and other screen sizes are supported by CSS but were not individually browser-tested. Instructions were checked against source, not replayed on a physical device during this website-only task.

No screenshots of old development UI were added to the guides. The instructions use descriptive entry points and the English labels present in the app. Future screenshot additions should be captured from the released version and placed beside the step they explain.
