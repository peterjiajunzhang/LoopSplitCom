SHARED EXPENSE GUIDES

An app to split recurring bills with roommates or a partner

Track recurring shared bills on iPhone and Android with LoopSplit. Save the schedule and split, review due periods, and record repayments.

By LoopSplit · Updated November 12, 2026 · For the released 1.2 series

LoopSplit is a bill-splitting app for iPhone, iPad and Android that tracks recurring shared bills as Loops. Save the schedule, payer and split for rent, internet or subscriptions, then review each due period in To Do before recording it. Each free Book allows 3 active Loops; Pro removes that limit.

Set up the bill once; review it each period

  1. Create a shared Book and invite your roommates or partner.
  2. Create a Loop with the schedule, payer, participants and agreed split.
  3. When a period is due, open To Do and check the amount before recording it.
  4. Review the synced balances. Record a repayment after members pay one another outside the app.

A Loop saves a recurring arrangement. A due period is a task to review, not an automatic bank payment. Check the period history before recording the same bill again.

Example: monthly rent and internet

Alex pays USD 1,800 rent and USD 60 internet each month. Alex and Sam split both equally: (1,800 + 60) ÷ 2 = USD 930 each. After both expenses sync, Sam owes Alex USD 930 if there are no other entries. When Sam pays Alex, record a repayment instead of another shared expense.

What if the electricity bill changes?

The schedule can repeat while the amount varies. Check the bill and enter the actual amount when recording the period. A receipt photo can stay attached to the expense. Pro also supports estimated expenses: a saved, synced estimate already affects balances, and confirming its final amount updates the same entry.

Free limits and what LoopSplit does

Shared expense tracking and repayments are free, without ads or daily expense limits. Each free Book supports 3 active Loops. Pro adds unlimited active Loops and estimated expenses. Members can share the same Book across Android, iPhone and iPad. LoopSplit records costs and repayments; it does not connect to your bank, pay providers or transfer money.
