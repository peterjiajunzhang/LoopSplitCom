SHARED EXPENSE GUIDES

Scan a receipt, fill in the details and split the expense

LoopSplit suggests a receipt’s amount, merchant and date with on-device scanning on iPhone and Android. Review the details and choose the split.

By LoopSplit · Updated November 12, 2026 · For the released 1.2 series

LoopSplit is a shared expense app for iPhone, iPad and Android with receipt scanning that helps fill the amount, merchant and date when available. Pro scans automatically; free accounts can request 3 scans total across devices. Review the suggestions, choose the payer and split, then save. Scanning does not assign individual receipt items to people.

From receipt photo to shared expense

  1. Use Scan or the expense form’s receipt area to take or select a clear receipt photo and choose the Book when prompted.
  2. With a free scan available, choose the action to fill the amount from the receipt. Pro scans automatically.
  3. Check the suggested total, merchant and date against the receipt, and check the Book’s currency. Correct any missing or incorrect details.
  4. Choose the actual payer, participants and split. Review everyone’s share before saving.

Recognition helps fill the form; it does not save an expense for you. A receipt with no readable amount can still be attached, and you can enter the details manually.

Example: a USD 90 shared receipt

Alex paid a USD 90 meal for Alex, Sam and Jo. After checking the scanned total and choosing an equal split, each person’s share is USD 30. Once the expense syncs, Sam and Jo each owe Alex USD 30 if there are no other entries. If the receipt excludes a pending tip, review the total rather than assuming it is final; Pro supports estimated expenses you confirm later.

Free photos, 3 free scans, automatic scanning with Pro

Attaching and viewing receipt photos is free. Each LoopSplit account has 3 free scans total across devices, not 3 per day or month. Free scanning requires a connection to confirm the shared allowance. Pro adds automatic, unlimited scanning and cropping. Ordinary shared expense tracking remains free, without ads or daily expense limits.

On-device recognition and shared attachments

Text recognition runs on your device, using Apple Vision on iPhone and iPad and Google ML Kit on Android; the image is not sent to a cloud OCR service. A receipt saved as a shared attachment is uploaded to the Book so its members can view it. This is form assistance, not item-by-item allocation, automatic tip calculation, currency conversion or a bank payment.
