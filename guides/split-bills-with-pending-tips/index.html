SHARED EXPENSE GUIDES

Split a restaurant bill while the final tip is still pending

Record an estimated shared expense in LoopSplit, then confirm the final total including the tip. See how the same entry and balances update.

By LoopSplit · Updated November 12, 2026 · For the released 1.2 series

LoopSplit supports shared expenses whose final amount is still pending, including a restaurant bill with a tip that posts later. With Pro, save an estimated total and the split now, then confirm the final total on the same expense. The estimate already counts toward balances; it is not a draft or a bank transaction status.

Example: USD 120 estimated, USD 132 final

Alex pays for a meal shared equally with Sam and Jo. Alex records an estimated total of USD 120, so each person’s share is USD 40. The final total, including the tip, is USD 132: confirming it changes each share to USD 44. If there are no other entries or repayments, Sam and Jo each owe Alex USD 44. The total increased by USD 12, or USD 4 per person; do not add a second USD 132 expense.

Record now, confirm the total later

  1. Create an expense and enter your best estimate of the total, including the expected tip.
  2. Enable the estimated-amount option, then choose the payer, participants and a supported split. Review and save.
  3. When the final amount is known, open the estimated expense in To Do or its details and choose the final-amount confirmation action.
  4. Enter the final total, not just the extra tip. Review and save the same expense.

After saving and syncing, the estimate affects balances and remains in To Do until confirmed. Confirmation updates its split and balances and completes that task. This example assumes no other transactions; check the Book’s current balances before settling.

Which split methods can use an estimate?

Estimated expenses support equal, percentage and shares splits. Exact-amount splits cannot be estimated. Creating an estimated expense requires Pro; an existing pending expense can be confirmed by a Book member without requiring their own Pro subscription. Control names follow your app language and version.

What “pending” means here

You mark the amount as not final and confirm it yourself. LoopSplit does not monitor a card or bank account, detect when a tip posts, calculate the tip for you or transfer money. A known final bill can be recorded as an ordinary shared expense for free, with no ads or daily expense limits.
