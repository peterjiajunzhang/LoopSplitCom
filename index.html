RECURRING BILLS

Rent and utilities, on schedule.

Set up weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly bills once. Fixed amounts are added for you; bills that vary wait for this period’s amount.

BALANCES

Know who owes whom.

Every expense updates everyone’s balance. Suggested payments help you settle up in fewer transfers, then record them in a tap.

FLEXIBLE SPLITS

Split it your way.

Split equally, by shares, by percentage or by exact amounts. Record refunds and other money coming in, too.

INVITE

OFFLINE
