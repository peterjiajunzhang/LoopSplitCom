LESS ADMIN. MORE LIVING.

From the first receipt
to the last repayment.

One shared Book for your household, a weekend away or the people you split costs with.

01

Add it once.

Record an expense or scan a receipt for amount, merchant and date suggestions. Review the details before you save.

02

Keep it on a Loop.

Save a schedule, payer and split for recurring costs. When a period is due, check the amount in To Do and record it.

03

Settle up together.

See everyone’s balance and suggested repayments. Pay outside the app, then record the payment to update the Book.

SHARED COSTS, CLEARER

Rent comes around.
The guesswork doesn’t have to.

Keep rent, internet and subscriptions together. See upcoming costs, estimated monthly totals and your share, then check off each due period in To Do.

When it’s time to settle up, everyone sees the same balances. LoopSplit records repayments; it never transfers money or connects to your bank.

RECURRING COSTS

Put recurring costs on a Loop.

Set up rent, utilities and subscriptions with a saved schedule and split. When a period is due, it appears in To Do for someone in the Book to check and record. Fixed and changing amounts stay under your control.

TO DO

Know what to do next.

Due Loops and estimated amounts wait in To Do until someone in the Book records or confirms them. Nothing is added without a look.

BALANCES

Know who owes whom.

Every expense updates everyone’s balance. Suggested payments show a way to settle up. Pay outside the app, then record the repayment.

FLEXIBLE SPLITS

Split it your way.

Split equally, by shares, by percentage or by exact amounts. Record refunds and other money coming in, too.

INVITE

Bring everyone in.

Choose people you’ve shared Books with, or invite someone new with a link, code or QR code. New people accept an invitation before joining.

OFFLINE

Add expenses anywhere.

No signal on the trip? New expenses are saved on your device and sync when you’re back online.

RECEIPTS

Scan it. Check it. Save it.

Read the amount and get merchant and date suggestions when available, using on-device Apple Vision. Try 3 free scans total per account, shared across devices. Attaching and viewing receipt photos stays free.

LOOPSPLIT PRO

More when you need it.

Each free Book has up to three active Loops. Pro adds automatic, unlimited receipt scanning, receipt cropping, unlimited Loops and estimated expenses you confirm later. If one member has Pro, everyone in that Book can use its Pro features there. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available; local prices and trial eligibility appear before purchase.

PRIVATE

Your data stays yours.

No ads or cross-app tracking. On iPhone and iPad, receipt recognition runs on your device. Photos saved as attachments are shared with your Book. Read our Privacy Policy for details, including Android SDK diagnostics.
