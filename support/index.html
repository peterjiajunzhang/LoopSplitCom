Getting started

Is LoopSplit free?

Yes. LoopSplit is free, with no ads or daily expense limits. Each free Book can have up to three active Loops, and everyone can attach receipt photos. Optional LoopSplit Pro subscriptions add more; see the next question.

What does LoopSplit Pro add?

Pro unlocks unlimited active Loops for everyone in your Books, estimated expenses for amounts that aren’t final yet (like a dinner whose tip posts later) that stay in To Do until you confirm them, and receipt cropping. Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly. Prices and any trial eligibility are shown in the app before purchase. It renews automatically until you cancel in your Apple Account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Subscriptions are covered by Apple’s standard Terms of Use.

Who gets notified when I add an expense?

The other people in that expense. You’re never notified about your own changes. Choose what you hear about in Account → Notifications.

Does LoopSplit move money?

No. LoopSplit keeps track of who paid and who owes. Pay each other however you like, then tap Record on the Balances screen so everyone’s balance updates.

How do I invite someone to a Book?

Open the Book and tap the person icon at the top. Share the invite link, or let them scan the QR code or type the 8-character code under + → Join with Invite Code. They sign in, confirm the Book, and they’re in. If someone already uses LoopSplit, you can also add them by email; they get a notification.

I didn’t get the verification email.

Check your spam or junk folder. On the Verify Your Email screen, tap Resend Email. If you typed the wrong address, choose Use a Different Account and sign up again.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap “Forgot Password?”. We’ll email you a link to set a new one.

Loops and balances

How do Loops work?

A Loop saves a recurring cost’s schedule, payer and split, such as rent or internet. When a period is due, it appears in To Do. Open it, check this period’s amount and save to record an expense. Saving does not send money. For amounts that change, like electricity, enter this period’s amount. Open the Loop’s History to see its recorded expenses.

Can I attach a receipt?

Yes. When you add or edit an expense, tap Receipt to take a photo or choose one from your library. Receipt photos are free for everyone, and everyone in the Book can view them. Cropping a receipt needs Pro.

How are suggested payments worked out?

LoopSplit adds up everyone’s share in the book and suggests payments that bring every balance back to zero, using as few transfers as it can.

Can I record a refund?

Yes. Tap Add, switch to Income and choose who received the money and who it’s shared with.

Which currencies are supported?

Each book uses one currency, chosen when you create it. Use a separate book for each currency.

Does LoopSplit work offline?

You can add expenses and income without a connection. They’re saved on your device and sync when you’re back online. Editing, deleting, recording payments and changing Loops need a connection.

Your account

How do I leave a Book?

On the home screen, swipe left on the Book and tap Leave. Your past expenses stay in the Book so everyone’s balances remain correct.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Delete Account. Your profile and photo are removed and you leave every book. This can’t be undone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
