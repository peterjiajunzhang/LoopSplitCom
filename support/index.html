Getting started

Is LoopSplit free?

Yes. Adding expenses, splitting, balances, payments, notifications and reminders are free, with no ads and no daily limits. A free book runs up to 3 recurring bills at a time.

What does LoopSplit Pro add?

Pro removes the 3-bill limit for everyone in your books, and lets you save an expense whose amount isn’t final yet, like a dinner whose tip posts later, and confirm it when it does. Pro is $0.99 a month or $6.99 a year. It renews automatically until you cancel in your Apple Account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Subscriptions are covered by Apple’s standard Terms of Use.

Who gets notified when I add an expense?

The other people in that expense. You’re never notified about your own changes. Choose what you hear about in Account → Notifications.

Does LoopSplit move money?

No. LoopSplit keeps track of who paid and who owes. Pay each other however you like, then tap Record on the Balances screen so everyone’s balance updates.

How do I invite someone to a book?

Open the book and tap the Invite People button at the top. Share the invite link, or let them scan the QR code or type the 8-character code under + → Join with Invite Code. They sign in, confirm, and they’re in. If someone already uses LoopSplit, you can also add them by email.

I didn’t get the verification email.

Check your spam or junk folder. On the Verify Your Email screen, tap Resend Email. If you typed the wrong address, choose Use a Different Account and sign up again.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap “Forgot Password?”. We’ll email you a link to set a new one.

Bills and balances

How do recurring bills work?

Add a bill once, such as rent or internet, and choose how often it repeats. When a fixed-amount bill is due, LoopSplit adds the expense for you. For bills that change, like electricity, it shows the bill under To Record so you can enter this period’s amount.

How are suggested payments worked out?

LoopSplit adds up everyone’s share in the book and suggests payments that bring every balance back to zero, using as few transfers as it can.

Can I record a refund?

Yes. In Add Expense, switch to Income and choose who received the money and who it’s shared with.

Which currencies are supported?

Each book uses one currency, chosen when you create it. Use a separate book for each currency.

Does LoopSplit work offline?

You can add expenses and income without a connection. They’re saved on your phone and sync when you’re back online. Editing, deleting, recording payments and changing bills need a connection.

Your account

How do I leave a book?

On the home screen, swipe left on the book and tap Leave. Your past expenses stay in the book so everyone’s balances remain correct.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Delete Account. Your profile and photo are removed and you leave every book. This can’t be undone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
