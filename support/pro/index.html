Free features & LoopSplit Pro

Understand limits, shared benefits and App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

On this pageWhat can I do for free?What does Pro add?Subscribe through your app storeRestore a purchaseManage or cancel a subscriptionWhat if Pro ends?

What can I do for free?

  • Create shared Books and record expenses without ads or daily expense limits.
  • Split costs, record income and repayments, and see shared balances.
  • Use up to 3 active Loops per free Book.
  • Attach and view receipt photos.
  • Try 3 receipt scans total per account, shared across devices.

What does Pro add?

  • Automatic, unlimited receipt scanning and receipt cropping.
  • Unlimited active Loops.
  • Estimated expenses that you confirm when the final amount is known.

If one member has active Pro, everyone in that Book can use its Pro features there. Membership in that Book does not give every member a separate personal subscription or unlock unrelated Books.

Subscribe through your app store

  1. Open Account and the LoopSplit Pro upgrade screen.
  2. Review the monthly or yearly option, local price, billing period and any trial offer shown.
  3. Confirm the purchase through Apple on iPhone or iPad, or through Google Play on Android, if you want to subscribe.

Pro offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Any free trial or introductory offer depends on what the store currently offers and your eligibility. Check the purchase screen for the exact trial length, price and renewal terms before you confirm.

Before confirming a purchase or account-linking step, check the LoopSplit account shown on screen. Pro stays linked to that LoopSplit account; Book sharing does not give each member a separate subscription.

Restore a purchase

  1. Use the Apple Account or Google Play account associated with the purchase and sign in to the LoopSplit account used for that subscription.
  2. Connect to the internet and open the Pro screen.
  3. Choose Restore Purchases and wait for the result.

What happens: the app checks the purchase and restores eligible access. Restore does not create another subscription. If your subscription belongs to another LoopSplit account, sign in to that account rather than purchasing again.

Manage or cancel a subscription

For an Apple subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LoopSplit on your iPhone or iPad and manage or cancel there. Apple subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, including the end of a trial. Apple shows your current access and renewal dates.

For a Google Play subscription, open Google Play Subscriptions with the Google account used to buy Pro, then select LoopSplit. You can also choose Manage Subscription on the Android Pro screen. Google Play shows the current billing and renewal details.

Deleting the app, leaving a Book or deleting your LoopSplit account does not cancel a subscription. Manage billing in the store where you subscribed; do not buy a second subscription to restore access on another device.

What if Pro ends?

Books without another active Pro member return to the free active-Loop limit. Loops over that limit can wait for Pro; pause another Loop to stay within the free limit, or restore active Pro access. Recorded expenses and balances remain.

If an active purchase still does not restore, contact support with the app version and the message you see. Do not send passwords or full payment details.
